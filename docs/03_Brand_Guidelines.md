# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name

Canadian Prospects Recruitment

## Nav Brand

Use:

`CANADIAN PROSPECTS RECRUITMENT`

Tagline:

`FINDING OPPORTUNITY. BUILDING FUTURES.`

## Logo Direction

The navigation uses a CPR circular logo treatment. The logo should feel athletic, direct, and badge-like. It should be readable at small sizes and suitable for use in headers, social graphics, and player cards.

## Colors

Primary red:

`#CC0000`

Primary black:

`#0A0A0A`

Supporting neutrals:

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted text: `#A7A7A7`
- Panel: `#141414`
- Strong panel: `#1F1F1F`

## Visual Style

- Dark, confident, high contrast
- Basketball-first imagery
- Red accents for actions, icons, stats, and highlights
- White sections may be used for education, process, and success stories
- Avoid orange and yellow accents
- Avoid football imagery

## Typography

- Bold uppercase headings for major campaign language
- Clear sans-serif body copy
- Tight but readable section titles
- No negative letter spacing

## Voice

The brand voice should be:

- Motivational
- Clear
- Direct
- Family-friendly
- Coach-aware
- Outcome-focused

## Copy Rules

- Use simple punctuation
- Do not use em dashes
- Keep calls to action short
- Use active language
- Focus on opportunity, preparation, visibility, and fit
